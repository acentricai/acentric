## The snapshot

- Step's working memory, rewritten by every step, so it stays small.

## The log

- Entries appended by steps, written once, numbered and timestamped by the runner.
- Where history and anything large belongs.

## The window

- How much of the log a step sees, chosen by `window(snapshot)`.
