## What it asks of you

- Splitting I/O into effects and handlers.
- Keeping everything JSON.
- A snapshot rewritten every step, so its size matters.
- `reissue` handlers that may run twice, so idempotency matters.

## When a loop is better

- A short script with no need to survive, stop, test or upgrade.
